# SalesMind AI

SalesForce AI

Build a modern, premium, AI-powered CRM called SalesMind AI. This is not a traditional CRM. It should feel like an intelligent AI employee that helps sales teams close more deals with minimal effort.

Design Philosophy

The design should be:

Clean

Minimal

Premium

Modern

Extremely fast

Mobile-first

Desktop optimized

Consumer-grade UI (similar polish to Linear, Notion, Arc Browser, and WhatsApp)

No clutter

Lots of whitespace

Focus on readability

The application should feel effortless to use. Every screen should have a clear purpose and no unnecessary elements.

Overall Feeling

The product should feel like the future of CRM.

It should not resemble traditional CRMs such as Salesforce or Zoho.

Instead, it should feel like an AI workspace that happens to manage customers.

Every screen should answer one question:

"How can AI help the salesperson close the next deal?"

Prioritize simplicity over feature overload. Hide advanced functionality behind progressive disclosure. Keep the interface calm, clean, and intelligent while making the AI assistant the central experience of the product.
